# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project
- Showing empathy towards other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Using this project or its issue tracker to facilitate unlawful surveillance, harassment, or identification of individuals without lawful authority and consent
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope of Responsible Use

This project performs biometric identification. Beyond ordinary community conduct, we ask that contributors and users:

- Not use this project to target, track, or identify individuals without lawful authority and appropriate consent
- Not represent this tool's output as a definitive or courtroom-grade identification
- Disclose limitations and failure modes honestly in any derivative work or deployment

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainer via the contact information on [@Amaan9136](https://github.com/Amaan9136)'s GitHub profile. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and to ban temporarily or permanently any contributor for behaviors they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.
